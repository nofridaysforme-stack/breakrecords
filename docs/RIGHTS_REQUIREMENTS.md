# What each organization asks for

The prepared documents (`packages/core/src/documents.ts`) follow this page: one form for each organization (PRO registration, MLC registration, SoundExchange registration, SoundExchange Letter of Direction, Copyright worksheets and the album group), plus the split sheet and label copy. Each fact below was read on the organization's own site on **Nov 9, 2026** or **Nov 18, 2026** where marked, unless it's marked *not confirmed*. Re-check a source before changing a document, and update the date.

The platform prepares; a person submits. Memberships and logins belong to the writer, publisher or label, and filings never happen without a person (CLAUDE.md).

## Performing rights (PROs)

**ASCAP** ([Registering your music FAQ](https://www.ascap.com/help/registering-your-music))
- Register in Member Access ("Register a Work") or the ASCAP mobile app.
- Writer shares add up to **50%** and publisher shares to **50%**, 100% in total. The split between collaborators is up to them; ASCAP encourages agreeing it in writing.
- A writer who is also the publisher of their own works is paid both shares.
- Register a work once; it appears in every listed party's catalog. If the work isn't self-published, ASCAP prefers the publisher registers it.
- A co-writer in a different PRO must register in that PRO too.
- Producers who contributed to the composition are listed as writers.
- Add performers once the song is performed. Versions with the same writers and shares are one work; alternate titles cover renamed versions.
- Songs with samples need both the master owner's and the composition owners' permission, and agreed shares.

**BMI**: bmi.com blocks automated reading. *Not confirmed on bmi.com (read in third-party guides and BMI news pages, Nov 18, 2026):* register in Online Services › Works Registration › Add New Work, in steps Work Info, Other Works, Publishers & Writers, Artists & Recordings, Summary. BMI counts writer shares to 100% and publisher shares to 100% (a 200% basis); with no publisher, "Manuscript" stands in. BMI keeps the shares from the first registration it receives, so each co-publisher should register too. Check these on the live form before relying on them.

**SESAC, GMR, others**: *not confirmed.* Writers in these PROs get the same packet and are told to register through their own PRO.

## Mechanical rights: The MLC

([How it works](https://www.themlc.com/how-it-works), [Membership](https://www.themlc.com/membership), [Tools](https://www.themlc.com/tools), [Songwriter Hub](https://www.themlc.com/songwriter-hub))
- Collects US digital audio mechanical royalties from streaming and download services under the blanket license, and pays Members monthly.
- Members are self-administered songwriters, publishers and administrators, and collective management organizations. Writers whose songs are managed by a publisher use the Songwriter Hub instead (view, export, request writer-name or IPI updates).
- Ownership shares total 100%. Shares over 100% are an **overclaim**, resolved with the Overclaims Tool.
- Check the **Claiming Tool** first: if the song is already registered, claim your share instead of registering it again.
- Register new songs with the **Works Registration Tools**: individual registration, bulk registration and CWR.
- Connect recordings (ISRCs) to songs with the **Matching Tool**.
- **Individual registration** ([How to register works](https://help.themlc.com/en/support/how-to-register-works-in-the-mlc-portal), read Nov 18, 2026), in six steps: (1) search by at least two criteria (work title, writer name) so you don't create a duplicate; claim remaining shares on a match, or file an overclaim if they're full; (2) work details, where only the Work Title is required, and an alternative title only for a language variation, a live variation, or a remix with the same writers; (3) writers and roles, found by name or IPI and created only if not found, with at least one Composer/Author or Composer role on every new registration; (4) publishers and collection shares: the publisher is the "original publisher"; a self-administered songwriter without a publishing entity uses their Member as the publisher; each collection share is between 0.01% and 100%; (5) recordings, optional, with Recording Title and Recording Artist Name required for each; (6) review and submit, which gives a reference number and a Submitted status.
- **Bulk registration** ([How do I complete the Bulk Work document?](https://help.themlc.com/en/support/how-do-i-complete-the-bulk-work-document), read Nov 18, 2026): the template is downloaded from the Member Hub (Bulk Registration Tool). Required columns are marked with an asterisk. Paired columns: AKA Title and AKA Title Type Code (E, F), Administrator Name and IPI (O, P), Recording Title and Artist (R, S). One work per title; extra writers go on the rows below, leaving columns A to F empty and starting at G. Writer Role Code is column J: AD, AR, A, C, CA, SR, SA, TR, PA. Publisher IPI is column M; a self-administered songwriter without one can't use bulk upload. Collection Share is column Q and can't be zero. Enter only shares you control. At most 300 rows, saved as .xlsx, columns never moved. *Not read:* the other columns (B to D, H, I, K, L, N, T onward); the template's Field Definitions tab has them, so the form's spreadsheet only fills the columns above.

## Digital radio: SoundExchange

([For artists, labels and producers](https://www.soundexchange.com/what-we-do/for-artists-labels-and-producers/), [Register](https://www.soundexchange.com/register/))
- Recording artists and sound recording owners must each register to be paid for non-interactive digital radio (SiriusXM, Pandora, iHeart Radio). Registration is free.
- After registering, creators search and claim their recordings and track payments in **SoundExchange Direct** (web or app). ISRC Search is free.
- Artists can send a share to producers, mixers or engineers with a **Letter of Direction**.
- **Submitting recordings** *(not confirmed on soundexchange.com; read in third-party guides, Nov 18, 2026)*: artist name, song title and ISRC; a claim basis (copyright owner, or authorized collection designee); at least one release; optional recording date, country of recording and owner nationality. The current templates are in SoundExchange Direct under Repertoire.
- **Letters of Direction** ([Guide to Featured Artist LODs](https://www.soundexchange.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/SoundExchange-Guide-to-Letters-of-Direction-8.10.2022.pdf), Aug 2022, read Nov 18, 2026): a featured artist directs SoundExchange to pay part of their royalties to a creative participant (producer, engineer, mixer, re-mixer), never to a featured artist, session musician, background singer, label, lender or advance company, and to a company only if the participant solely owns it. With several featured artists, each signs their own LOD. The letter gives the featured artist, the performers' legal names, the effective date (any date, past or future, or a box for all royalties received), the performers' signatures or their authorized signatory's, and the payee's name, address, telephone and email. The repertoire chart lists Solo Artist or Group Name, Sound Recording Track Name, Effective Date, ISRC and Percentage, a percentage of the featured artist's share, never points. An electronic signature needs the signing service's certificate of completion. Send to accounts@soundexchange.com or through SoundExchange Direct; it takes at least two weeks. Payees need a W-9 or W-8 on file in SoundExchange Direct or face backup withholding (SoundExchange, Dec 2019).

## Copyright: US Copyright Office

([Fees](https://www.copyright.gov/about/fees.html), [Performing arts registration](https://www.copyright.gov/registration/performing-arts/))
- Fees: Standard Application **$65**; Single Application (one author, same claimant, one work, not for hire) **$45**; group of works published on an album of music **$65**; paper forms $125.
- Music and lyrics are works of the performing arts; sound recordings are registered separately or with the album group option.
- The worksheets pick the application: a Single Application only for one author who is also the claimant (not made for hire); otherwise a Standard Application; and a release-level worksheet for the group of works on an album of music. *Not confirmed:* the group option's eligibility questions; the worksheet tells the person to answer them on the form.
- *Not confirmed (from the Office's long-standing application, not re-read):* the application asks for the type of work, title, year of completion, publication date and nation, each author's name, citizenship or domicile, whether it was made for hire and what they created, the claimant's name and address, a transfer statement when the claimant isn't the author, and a deposit copy.

## Recording IDs: ISRC

([US ISRC Agency](https://usisrc.org/))
- Each ISRC permanently identifies one recording, whatever format it appears on.
- The owner assigns ISRCs from a **Rights Owner Prefix**, a one-time **$95** fee, with no further cost. Distributors also assign them.

## Split sheets

No organization page was read for this. The split sheet carries the song's title, alternate titles and ISWC, and for each writer: legal name, what they wrote, PRO, IPI, publisher and share. Shares total exactly 100%, and every writer signs.
