# What each organization asks for

The prepared documents (`packages/core/src/documents.ts`) follow this page. Each fact below was read on the organization's own site on **Nov 9, 2026**, unless it's marked *not confirmed*. Re-check a source before changing a document, and update the date.

The platform prepares; a person submits. Memberships and logins belong to the writer, publisher or label, and filings never happen without a person (CLAUDE.md).

## Performing rights (PROs)

**ASCAP** ([Registering your music FAQ](https://www.ascap.com/help/registering-your-music))
- Register in Member Access ("Register a Work") or the ASCAP mobile app.
- Writer shares add up to **50%** and publisher shares to **50%**, 100% in total. The split between collaborators is up to them; ASCAP encourages agreeing it in writing.
- A writer who is also the publisher of their own works is paid both shares.
- Register a work once; it appears in every listed party's catalog. If the work isn't self-published, ASCAP prefers the publisher registers it.
- A co-writer in a different PRO must register in that PRO too.
- Producers who contributed to the composition are listed as writers.
- Add performers once the song is performed. Versions with the same writers and shares are one work; alternate titles cover renamed versions.
- Songs with samples need both the master owner's and the composition owners' permission, and agreed shares.

**BMI**: bmi.com blocks automated reading. *Not confirmed:* the documents use the same writer and publisher halves, and send BMI writers to their BMI account to register.

**SESAC, GMR, others**: *not confirmed.* Writers in these PROs get the same packet and are told to register through their own PRO.

## Mechanical rights: The MLC

([How it works](https://www.themlc.com/how-it-works), [Membership](https://www.themlc.com/membership), [Tools](https://www.themlc.com/tools), [Songwriter Hub](https://www.themlc.com/songwriter-hub))
- Collects US digital audio mechanical royalties from streaming and download services under the blanket license, and pays Members monthly.
- Members are self-administered songwriters, publishers and administrators, and collective management organizations. Writers whose songs are managed by a publisher use the Songwriter Hub instead (view, export, request writer-name or IPI updates).
- Ownership shares total 100%. Shares over 100% are an **overclaim**, resolved with the Overclaims Tool.
- Check the **Claiming Tool** first: if the song is already registered, claim your share instead of registering it again.
- Register new songs with the **Works Registration Tools**: individual registration, bulk registration and CWR.
- Connect recordings (ISRCs) to songs with the **Matching Tool**.
- Field-by-field requirements are in The MLC's help center (another site, not read). *Not confirmed:* the packet lists title, ISWC, writers with IPI and role, owners with IPI and share, and ISRCs.

## Digital radio: SoundExchange

([For artists, labels and producers](https://www.soundexchange.com/what-we-do/for-artists-labels-and-producers/), [Register](https://www.soundexchange.com/register/))
- Recording artists and sound recording owners must each register to be paid for non-interactive digital radio (SiriusXM, Pandora, iHeart Radio). Registration is free.
- After registering, creators search and claim their recordings and track payments in **SoundExchange Direct** (web or app). ISRC Search is free.
- Artists can send a share to producers, mixers or engineers with a **Letter of Direction**.
- *Not confirmed:* the exact catalog fields an owner submits. The packet gives ISRC, title, featured artist, owner, ℗ year, length, genre, release, UPC and release date.

## Copyright: US Copyright Office

([Fees](https://www.copyright.gov/about/fees.html), [Performing arts registration](https://www.copyright.gov/registration/performing-arts/))
- Fees: Standard Application **$65**; Single Application (one author, same claimant, one work, not for hire) **$45**; group of works published on an album of music **$65**; paper forms $125.
- Music and lyrics are works of the performing arts; sound recordings are registered separately or with the album group option.
- *Not confirmed (from the Office's long-standing application, not re-read):* the application asks for the type of work, title, year of completion, publication date and nation, each author's name, citizenship or domicile, whether it was made for hire and what they created, the claimant's name and address, a transfer statement when the claimant isn't the author, and a deposit copy.

## Recording IDs: ISRC

([US ISRC Agency](https://usisrc.org/))
- Each ISRC permanently identifies one recording, whatever format it appears on.
- The owner assigns ISRCs from a **Rights Owner Prefix**, a one-time **$95** fee, with no further cost. Distributors also assign them.

## Split sheets

No organization page was read for this. The split sheet carries the song's title, alternate titles and ISWC, and for each writer: legal name, what they wrote, PRO, IPI, publisher and share. Shares total exactly 100%, and every writer signs.
